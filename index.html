The details

What is in the beta today.

Frames

  • iPhone, portrait and landscape
  • iPhone 16, 17 and 18 models, including 16e, 17e and iPhone Air, portrait and landscape
  • iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad and iPad mini, portrait and landscape
  • iPhone Duo open, portrait and landscape
  • iPhone Duo closed, portrait and landscape
  • Black, Silver, Gold and Blue finishes
  • Picked automatically to match your device and posture, or pin the one you want
  • Move, resize, rotate, flip and tilt the device, with full shadow control

Backdrops and export

  • Aurora, Sunset, Graphite, transparent, or your own photo
  • Auto size, or Full HD, Story/Reel/TikTok, Instagram square and portrait, YouTube thumbnail, link preview and X post
  • App Store sizes, including iPhone Duo inner and outer displays
  • Headline and subtitle above or below the device
  • Ready-made templates for the App Store, Instagram and Stories
  • Remembers your settings between launches
  • Save to Photos or share as a PNG

Device info

  • Model, system, display, battery, hardware and storage
  • Network, sensors, cameras, accessibility and locale
  • Fold and hinge status on iPhone Duo
  • Tells you if a newer iOS is available for your model
  • Search, and export as a PDF

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later
  • iPhone Duo features need iOS 27.1
  • Currently available through TestFlight
