Help with Framedevice

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you are a TestFlight tester, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app. Not testing yet? Join the beta on TestFlight. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing.

Common questions

Why is there no Fold & Hinge section on my phone?

That section only appears on a device that reports a hinge, which today means iPhone Duo. On other iPhones and iPads it is hidden.

Why does the device name show the model instead of my name?

Since iOS 16, apps can only read the name you gave your device if Apple has approved a special entitlement. Framedevice shows the model name (for example “iPhone Air”) and the system identifier instead.

Why can't I see battery health or cycle count?

iOS does not give apps access to battery health, capacity, cycle count or temperature. Those are only in Settings › Battery.

Camera or Photos access is not working.

Open the About tab and tap “Camera and Photos permissions” to jump to Settings. The camera is only needed for the Capture tab. Saving mockups needs add-only Photos access.

Can I use Apple's official device frames?

Framedevice draws its own frames. Apple's licence for its design resources does not allow bundling them inside an app, so the frames are original illustrations and will not match Apple's hardware pixel for pixel.

My export has the wrong size.

In the Export section of the Mockups tab, “Auto” shapes the image around the frame. Choose a preset such as Full HD or Instagram to get an exact pixel size, with the device centred inside it.
